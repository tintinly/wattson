"What's a day in the arena like for you?"

This is one of the top five questions I get in DMs. Well, it deserves its own post.

## 7:00 — Nikola Alarm

My cat steps on my face. Not the pillow, not a "meow" — he **steps directly on my face.**

"Nikola, not today. I have a match."

He ignores me.

## 8:00 — Croissant & Oscilloscope

Breakfast is a French croissant. Not the kind from a space station vending machine — homemade. Dad taught me that in an electrical engineer's kitchen, temperature and time are both parameters.

While eating, I check last night's soldered circuit board with the oscilloscope. Nikola curls up next to the bread basket, staring at the waveform like it's a bug.

## 10:00 — Arena Briefing

The Syndicate releases today's match menu. Map: World's Edge. Weather: Volcanic ash, moderate visibility.

Wraith gives me a look in the briefing room. That look means: "Don't stray too far from me."

## 12:00 — Arena Time

Drop. Land. Deploy nodes. Interception Pylon.

Today's opponent is a careless one — he was zoning out while dropping. His squad came in from the east, and my two fences pinned him in a crossfire. My teammates finished him off.

**The goal isn't to kill. The goal is to control space.**

*But he ran into my fence twice. I won't be polite about that.*

## 15:00 — Back to Base for Repairs

The communicator I got from Crypto was pierced by shrapnel. Head to the backyard for replacement parts. Found a hidden Nessie in the spare parts bin. Put it back where it belongs.

## 19:00 — Dinner Time

Lifeline says she found a new dumpling place. Gibraltar says he can eat forty dumplings. Bangalore says "stop bragging, last time you were full at thirty-two."

Wraith doesn't say much, but she ate twenty. She had the fewest dumplings — but she stayed the longest.

## 22:00 — Workbench

The arena lights go out. I sit at my workbench, a cup of cocoa in hand, a circuit board in front of me.

Nikola lies on my lap, tail slowly sweeping.

*Far away in some swamp, a Nessie waits under the moonlight for the next person to find it.*

That's my day. A day in the life of an arena legend. A day in the life of an electrical engineer. A day in the life of an ordinary girl — *if ordinary girls can catch grenades with their bare hands.*

> *"Life is a match without a ring. But I don't need a ring — I have current, friends, and a cat."*
