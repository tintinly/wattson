That night, I recreated my fence design on my workbench — a scaled-down version. I wanted to prove to Dad that the system could actually work.

But there was one problem: I didn't have a dome shield. I'd read Singh Labs' research reports though, and I knew they had one there.

I fought with Dad and stormed off deep into the forests of King's Canyon.

**A storm was coming that night.**

## The "Ghost" in the Lab

At Singh Labs, I found the components I needed — but I felt like someone was watching me. Strange black mist swirled in the air, like shadows with minds of their own.

I couldn't see what the "ghost" looked like. It moved too fast — faster than a person should be able to. But it didn't hurt me. It just watched, like it was making sure I wasn't a threat.

I was terrified. I lost my rubber-soled boots running out of the lab.

## Experiment Gone Wrong

I set up my miniature fence device in the forest. Dark clouds rolled in, wind howled — but all I could think about was proving myself right.

Then — **lightning struck my generator.**

Current burst in every direction. I was thrown backward into a tree. Flames consumed the surrounding forest. I tried to stand — but my body wouldn't obey. Pain spread from my fingertips to my spine like electricity.

I thought I was going to die there.

## Hands Through the Flames

At the edge of consciousness — a pair of hands, without a body, reached through the flames and grabbed me.

It was the ghost.

It carried me through the burning forest and laid me on the porch of Dad's cabin. Then it disappeared into the night. As if it had never existed.

I later learned: that was my first encounter with **Wraith**. She had seen my future through a rift in time — a friend she would need.

*Years later, when the chance came to say "thank you" to one of the most important people in my life, I would remember those hands.*

## After the Storm

I survived. Three broken bones, a concussion, and a lightning scar running from my neck down my arm.

The day Dad submitted the fence blueprints to the arena, he didn't mention my experiment in the storm. He didn't mention the ghost who saved his daughter that night.

He only said one thing:

> **"Natalie, this fence will always be your design."**

⚡ Wattson
