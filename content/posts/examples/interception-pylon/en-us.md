Do you know how many things can be thrown at your window?

Grenades. Arc Stars. Jump Mines. Thermite. Gibraltar's Defensive Bombardment. Bangalore's Rolling Thunder. Fuse's… *whatever he's got.*

Too many. So I made a solution.

## Design Principle

The core of the Interception Pylon is a **high-frequency electromagnetic field scanning system**. It emits thousands of detection pulses per second in a spherical radius, weaving an invisible electromagnetic safety net in the air.

Any high-energy object (explosives, shrapnel, incendiary devices) entering this area on a projectile trajectory will be, within 0.03 seconds:
1. Identified by trajectory
2. Interception point calculated
3. Neutralized by a directed EMP pulse

Intercepted munitions transform into **Charged Arc Stars** on the ground — see, free ammo!

## Additional Features

The Interception Pylon can also:
- **Recharge nearby shields** — excess energy released during interception feeds back into teammates' shield systems
- **Speed up tactical cooldowns** — the electromagnetic field slightly accelerates capacitor charging rates

## Personal Thoughts

I deliberately designed the Pylon to look like a totem pole — *wait, doesn't everyone like totem poles?*

Just kidding. Actually, I designed it this way to honor my father's engineering legacy. He taught me: **A good engineer doesn't just make things work — he makes them meaningful.**

Every time I place a Pylon in the arena and it hums to life in mid-air — I feel like Dad is right there watching me.

*Then three grenades explode under it and ruin the moment.*
