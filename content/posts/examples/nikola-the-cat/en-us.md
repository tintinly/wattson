I have two cats.

One is a gray tabby named **Nikola**. The other one — my teammate said he lost it, but I didn't tell him I let the cat go because cats shouldn't live in encrypted comms rooms.

…Okay, the first cat has a better story.

## The Name

Nikola Tesla — inventor of the AC electrical system. A genius who brought light to the world, yet died in obscurity.

My dad used to tell me stories about Tesla. He said: "Natalie, the greatest inventors aren't always the most famous. They're just the ones who care the most."

So I named my cat Nikola. *He probably doesn't care about AC, but he cares a lot about tuna.*

## A Cat's Daily Life

Nikola loves lying on my workbench watching me solder. His head follows the red glow of the soldering iron left and right, his tail slowly sweeping across the table — until it touches an unsecured wire, and *clatter*.

Then he gets startled. Then he stares at me with a "this is your fault" look.

## One Story

Once, I was debugging a prototype of the arena fence. Nikola jumped onto the console and stepped on a few keys.

Guess what happened?

He triggered a circuit efficiency bug I hadn't noticed before. That bug directly improved the fence system's charge speed by 12%.

*Yes, my cat made an original contribution to my career.*

I won't tell him that. He's already arrogant enough.

> *"Cats don't understand circuits, but they know exactly which keys to step on at the right moment."*

⚡ Wattson
