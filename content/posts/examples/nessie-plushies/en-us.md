If you find a little green dinosaur plushie in some corner of King's Canyon — **that was me.**

## Where Nessies Come From

My mother sewed many Nessie plushies before I was born. They come in different sizes — some small enough to fit in your palm, some big enough to be pillows. Every single one is hand-stitched, the stitches a little crooked, but each one is stuffed full of love.

After she left, these Nessies were my only connection to her.

You know what? When people talk about the Loch Ness Monster, they're not talking about a real creature. They're talking about "believing in the beauty of the unknown."

*I think my mom believed in that too.*

## Why I Hide Them in the Arena

The first time I went to King's Canyon with Dad for a survey, I secretly stuffed a Nessie into a tree hollow. I thought: "If someone stops in the middle of a fight and sees this little dinosaur — would they smile?"

The answer is: yes.

Later, more and more people started finding these Nessies. Like Bloodhound — they stepped on a Nessie while tracking an enemy, stopped for three seconds looking at it, and then gently placed it back where it was.

*Even Bloodhound spared my Nessie. That's power.*

## Fun Facts

Nessie locations in the arena include:
- Inside a hollow log in Swamp
- Under an abandoned trailer by the dam
- Beneath a loose floorboard in Skull Town ruins
- Behind an ammo crate at Artillery (this one is trickier!)
- In the wreckage of a crashed supply ship — don't ask how I got in there

I won't tell you all the spots. *Go find them. That's part of the treasure hunt.*

## If You Want to Sew One Too

Take an old towel, fold it into a dinosaur shape, and sew four stitches. It doesn't need to look perfect. What matters is thinking about the person you miss while you sew.
