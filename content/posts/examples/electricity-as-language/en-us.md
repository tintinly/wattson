Dad once told me: "Before you could speak, your eyes were already following the waveforms on the oscilloscope."

I'm not sure if that's true — but I know electricity was the first thing I truly *understood*.

## Voltage, Current, Resistance — My Way of Understanding

Many people find electricity hard to grasp. It's invisible, untouchable (seriously, don't touch it!), yet it exists.

Let me explain with my favorite analogy:

Imagine a river flowing through a valley —

- **Voltage (V)** is the height difference of the water surface, determining how badly the water wants to move. The bigger the drop, the stronger the push.
- **Current (I)** is the volume of water flowing past your feet. The greater the flow, the stronger the flush.
- **Resistance (R)** is the rocks and weeds in the river. They block the flow and slow it down.

Put these three together, and you get Ohm's Law: **V = I × R**

Voltage = Current × Resistance. Three variables, one equation. Simple, elegant, deterministic.

*That's why I love it.*

## Why You Should Care

Because anything that runs on batteries — your phone, your cat's automatic feeder, the arena's Interception Pylon — follows this equation.

Understand V=IR, and you grasp the foundation of the entire electronic world.
