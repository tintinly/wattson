He told me his real name in a voice so quiet, like he was afraid the walls would eavesdrop.

It was during a very ordinary dinner. No conspiracy, no agents — just one person deciding to tell another person **everything.**

## Before Trust

During the Broken Ghost mission, we assembled an artifact together. He didn't talk much — always staring at his drone's screen. But I noticed something: he would warn me before danger approached.

Not everyone — just me.

Then *someone* (I won't name the metal-skinned killing machine) hacked his drone and made everyone think he was a traitor. I was fooled too. I was angry.

*I was hurt.*

## Why I Trust Him

Because if someone were truly a traitor — they wouldn't still be kind to you when no one was watching.

He invited me to his rundown room and confessed everything: his real name was **Tae Joon Park**. He and his foster sister were framed for murdering a Syndicate VIP. He was on the run. He opposed everything the Syndicate stood for — and my entire life was part of the Syndicate.

My fence. My father's legacy. My first job as an adult. All of it.

I took about… twenty seconds to think.

Then I said: "I understand."

*Not "I agree." Not "I believe you." Just "I understand."*

## Now

Sometimes we chat late at night — about circuits, about encryption algorithms, about how to add electromagnetic interference modules to a drone. (The Syndicate doesn't know we're working on these.) He still speaks quietly, but around me, he doesn't need to pretend anymore.

*And I know I was the one who pressed the defibrillator that saved his foster mother.*
*And she knows the most common word in her son's messages is "Paquette."*

## Trust Is Not an Equation

Dad said my world could be simplified into equations. But trust isn't voltage. Trust has no repeatable experiment. Trust is a gamble — and you can only close your eyes and jump.

*I jumped. He jumped too.*

> *"We always keep secrets from each other, because we're all lonely people — until one day we discover that loneliness isn't mandatory."*
