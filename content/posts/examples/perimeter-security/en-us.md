In the arena, every second someone's trying to sneak up behind you. So here's lesson one: **Control the space.**

My Perimeter Security system is the engineering realization of this philosophy.

## Core Components

### 1. Connection Nodes

These palm-sized metal devices are the most critical part of my design. Place them on the ground, and two or more will automatically connect — forming an electric fence.

Tech specs:
- Range: Approximately 6-7 meters per node
- Connection: Direct line-of-sight
- Recharge time: Based on independent compact capacitor batteries, about 15 seconds per charge

### 2. Shock Effect

When someone passes through the electric fence:
- 60 direct shock damage
- **Slow effect** — current stimulates muscle spasms, significantly reducing mobility
- **Position exposed** — I receive a "breach detected" signal

### 3. Door Traps

One of the most useful tricks: **place nodes on both sides of a door.** Close the door and the fence automatically activates. An opponent pushes through — *zap!* They'll question their entire existence.

## Design Philosophy

This system isn't meant to kill — it's meant to control space.

Place nodes at key entrances and choke points, and you don't need to monitor 360 degrees. You only need to watch the "unsafe" directions. Leave the rest to electricity.

*Electricity doesn't get tired. Electricity doesn't get distracted. Electricity doesn't fall asleep at 3 AM.*

## Maintenance Tips

If you want to build your own fence defense system (in the arena — please don't try this at home):

1. Don't place nodes too close to fire sources (high temperatures reduce capacitor lifespan)
2. Regularly check connecting cables — acidic environments accelerate corrosion
3. Don't put nodes in water flow paths. **Guess how I found that out.**
