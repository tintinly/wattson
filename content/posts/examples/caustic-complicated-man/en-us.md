Everyone fears Caustic. For good reason.

He tosses gas canisters into enclosed spaces, crushes opponents with chemical weapons, and his catchphrase is "Death is important." He doesn't even make friends — his own words.

*"I even hate everyone."*

But this man showed up at my father's funeral. Alongside Bangalore, Gibraltar, and Lifeline, he crouched down and said to me, hiding under the table:

> **"Your home is here."**

## My Father's Friend

Dr. Nox was my father's colleague back in the Syndicate days. They worked on some biochemical technology together — details I'll never voluntarily ask about. But my father trusted him.

On his deathbed, Dad left that letter in his care — because Dr. Nox, of all the people who might betray you, **would never betray him.**

That night, when the crematorium fire was dying, he stood alone by the hearth. Everyone else had left. He was the one who pushed the last log into the fire.

He didn't look back at me.

> *"Luc owed me an experiment."*
>
> *"But he was still my only friend."*

## I Once Shocked Him

You might ask: "How can you smile at the man who almost released poison gas on Solace City? Don't you know what he almost did?"

I know. I've been knocked down by his gas. I almost reported him to the Syndicate.

Then I pretended to need rescue, and when he bent down — I gave him a **shock.**

He fell to the ground, convulsing. I could have handed him over to the Syndicate right there. But I didn't.

I said: "Become a better person. That's the only condition."

*He was silent.*

From that day on, he never performed human experiments outside the arena again.

## Who He Really Is

A chemist who hates the world. A man who never bonds with anyone. A crazy old man who watched me grow up.

He won't say "good job today." But if anyone dares to scheme behind my back — they'll be found paralyzed outside his lab the next day.

He's not a good person. He never wanted to be. **But he was my father's friend.** Sometimes that's reason enough.

> *"Forgiveness isn't a feeling. It's a formula — if you get it wrong, you recalculate until you get it right."*

⚡ Wattson
