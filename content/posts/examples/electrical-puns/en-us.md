*You knew this day was coming.*

Alright, here are my all-time favorite electrical puns. You can use them. You can also not use them. But if people roll their eyes when you do — **that's your problem.**

## 1. The Classic Intro

**"Killo-Watt-Son!"**

My signature opener. One word that contains my name, my profession, and a warning.

## 2. Victory Moment

**"You burned bright, but you burned out."**

Said to a defeated opponent while pulling nodes out of the ground.

## 3. The Counter-Kill

**"Tire as you might, you can't killo-watt-son!"**

I say this every time I pull off a counter-kill. Never gets old.

## 4. Current Philosophy

**"Electricity never forgets its direction."**

*Voltage is certain. Current is certain. Only the human heart wavers.*

## 5. Arena Truth

**"It's difficult to win when I conduct the power."**

What this really means: you can't beat me because I control the entire electrical system.

## 6. Short Circuit Warning

**"Don't blow a fuse. Get it?"**

Said while discharging electricity above an opponent's head.

## 7. Terminal Voltage

**"This is the end of your electrical current."**

Also means the end of your life. Double meaning. One phrase. *Perfect.*

## 8. Post-Match Wisdom

**"I'm the one with the stronger current."**

More current means more stopping power — it's both physics and social physics.

## 9. For the Team

**"My adversaries are in for a shock!"**

Said right before deploying my fence. My teammates know what that means — soon.

## 10. My Dad's Favorite

**"Sweetheart, when life gives you a short circuit — make sparks."**

This isn't in the game's voice lines. It's what my dad wrote on my social media bio — *wait, in 2727 was it called a "log" or a "feed"?*
