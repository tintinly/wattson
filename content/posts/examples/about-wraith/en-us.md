I've watched Wraith in the arena many times — her phase shift as fast as a blue flash of lightning. But that's not what I want to tell you about.

*I want to tell you about who she really is.*

## The Ghost and the Girl

The first time I met her, I couldn't see her. It was a stormy night when I was 15 — I got struck by lightning and lost consciousness in a burning forest. A pair of hands reached through the flames and pulled me back from the brink of death.

Back then, I thought I was hallucinating — a "ghost" saving me.

Years later, I learned that was during her time in the IMC ARES Division facility. From another dimension, she saw through a time rift — an injured girl and a future she couldn't ignore.

*Of all the versions of herself, she chose to reach out that hand.*

Without her, I wouldn't be alive to write this blog.

## Best Friend

Wraith isn't good with words. She won't text you asking "how was your day," but if you can't sleep at 3 AM, she'll sit beside you all night without saying a word.

Everyone else in the arena thinks she's cold, silent, unapproachable. But she's just… been through too much. She's seen too many versions of herself, too many bad endings. She stays calm because she knows just how bad things can get.

But with me — she smiles. A real smile. The kind with light in her eyes.

*I guess that's the one expression she shows to the same person across all timelines.*

## One Thing

I once asked her: "In all those dimensions, did you ever regret saving me?"

She looked at me like I was someone who didn't understand math.

"In every timeline," she said, "I would reach out for you."

> *"You think she's cold. You just haven't seen her reach her hands into the fire."*
