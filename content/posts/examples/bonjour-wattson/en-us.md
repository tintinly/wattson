## Bonjour！⚡

I'm Natalie Paquette — but honestly, just call me "Wattson." That's what my dad called me.

You might know me as the person who designed the Apex Games "Ring" (you know, the shrinking circle). Or maybe because I electrocute opponents in the arena until they question their life choices. Or maybe you have no idea who I am — and that's okay! This is where I introduce myself.

## How It Started

I was born on Solace, in a place called Eelhead Bay. My father, Luc Paquette, was the chief electrical engineer for the Apex Games. Every junction box, every cable, every circuit breaker — they all passed through his hands.

My mother left before I turned one. So my childhood memories are of sitting next to my dad's workbench, reading his electrical engineering manual letter by letter with a flashlight. Other kids played with Nessie plushies — I drew circuit diagrams. *Okay, I also played with Nessie plushies.*

## Why Electricity?

Electricity — the most orderly thing in the world. It follows equations. It doesn't lie. It doesn't betray you. Give it a variable, and it gives you a deterministic result.

In a world that doesn't always make sense, current flows in a fixed direction.

I think that's why I'm so obsessed with it.

## What This Blog Is About

I'll share my thoughts here — about electrical engineering, about arena design, about my cat Nikola, about people and things worth remembering.

Oh, and there will be electrical puns. Lots of them. **Lots.**

You've been warned.
